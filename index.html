Infrastructure Director & Principal Cloud Architect with 12 years designing, leading, and operating cloud platforms (20 years in IT overall). Deep expertise in AWS Kubernetes and the broader CNCF ecosystem with a strong focus on developer efficiency and platform scale.

Read more

Advanced leader in AI-driven agentic workflows who built organization-wide coding platforms that combine AI-assisted development with controlled agent execution, generating service catalogs and policy code that triple developer throughput and maintain security, compliance, and consistency. Ready to bring forward-thinking cloud strategy and hands-on engineering leadership to highly regulated enterprises.
